2. What Palcore is

Palcore is a free unofficial companion overlay for Palworld, distributed through the Overwolf client. It reads your local Palworld save files on this PC and, while Palworld is running, live position from Overwolf Game Events (GEP), to show your chests, base Pals, and a base radar.

Palcore does not operate a Palcore account system and does not run a Palcore backend that receives your save files.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Local Palcore preferences: kept on this PC until you clear Overwolf/app data or uninstall Palcore / Overwolf.
  • Palworld saves: remain in your game folders; Palcore reads them and does not keep a separate cloud copy.
  • Ads / Overwolf account: retained according to Overwolf’s retention practices.

To delete Palcore-controlled local data: uninstall Palcore and/or the Overwolf client, or remove the unpacked/installed app. That stops Palcore from reading saves.

To exercise rights in Overwolf/ad data: use Overwolf settings / CMP and Overwolf’s privacy contacts (including dpo@overwolf.com where applicable).

8. Children

Palcore is a gaming companion and is not directed at children. You must meet the minimum age required by Palworld, Steam/your store, and Overwolf. Do not use Palcore if you are below those ages. We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children.

9. International transfers

Palcore does not operate its own servers. Overwolf and ad partners may process data outside your country (including outside the EEA). Those transfers follow Overwolf’s and vendors’ safeguards.

10. Security

Saves are read locally through Overwolf file APIs. No method is 100% secure. Keep your Windows account and Overwolf client updated. Palcore is not a Palworld cheat tool and does not modify game memory to change gameplay.
